# Symbalics — Cognition-Plane Research Overview

**Status:** proposed, unvalidated architecture; distinct from both AMAS acronym expansions.
**Primary historical sources:** July 2026 AMAS–Symbalics Mathematical Specification v0.2; *AMAS–Symbalics Architecture and ATTICUS*, v2.6 Pass9C Reader Edition and v2.7 Pass9D reviewer materials. Unpublished manuscripts are not copied into this repository.

## Architectural hypothesis

A neural foundation model can be paired with persistent, explicitly typed symbolic state across multiple update timescales. A bounded recurrent workspace can consider competing interpretations and propagate corrections, while separately trusted governance mediates state promotion, memory and action.

## Conceptual subsystems

1. **Input and entry state:** source classification, candidate frames, task intent and correction-sensitive short-lived context.
2. **Typed symbolic representation:** objects, relations, provenance, epistemic categories, confidence and privacy flags; representations are proposals, not a proof of correctness.
3. **Multi-timescale stabilization:** distinct fast conversational state, episodic state and longitudinal state; durable promotion/demotion must discount repeated or correlated evidence.
4. **Competing cognitive workspace:** allocate bounded attention across hypotheses and intents; include caps or diversification to reduce frame monopoly.
5. **Hypothesis and uncertainty handling:** competing interpretations and unknown/open-set outcomes remain explicit; fiction, imagination, reported material and observation must remain distinguishable.
6. **Neural–symbolic coupling:** proposed retrieval, attention, routing or other bounded neural modulation; none is established superior to conventional retrieval or long-context baselines.
7. **Consolidation and governance:** cognitive proposals do not directly modify protected identity, canonical memory, model policy or external effects; AMAS-AUTH and trusted commit/action boundaries review them.

## Three planes (not three AMAS technologies)

`ATTICUS governance / AMAS integrity boundary / Symbalics cognition`

The proposed AMAS integrity boundary contains separately identified **AMAS-AUTH** (existing authority governance) and **AMAS-MANIP** (research manipulation analysis). Its job is to prevent model-generated considerations from becoming unauthorized state, published claims or external actions.

## Evidence and limits

The mathematical specification is a preliminary design sketch, not an integrated implementation, proof of AGI, consciousness, guaranteed safety, or demonstrated performance improvement. The research manuscript tracks established/adapted/proposed/open components separately. Experimental protocols and negative results are prerequisites for any stronger claim.

Technical equations, private research datasets, the complete reviewer book, and implementation-sensitive design packages are not incorporated in this public integration commit.
